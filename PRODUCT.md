# EP Talk

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

macOS

## Stack

Assumption from the native Mac brief: SwiftUI, Core Audio and Swift Package Manager, with a standalone app bundle.

## Users

The owner of a Teenage Engineering EP-2350 FX–MIC who dictates into a Mac with Handy or another hold-to-talk app.

## Product Purpose

Squeezing the mic holds a configured keyboard shortcut. Releasing the mic releases the shortcut so the dictation app can finish and transcribe.

## Operating Context

The EP-2350 runs on batteries and connects by its 3.5 mm coiled cable through the Sabrent AU-UCMA. The front cover stays on during use. Acoustic control signals share the audio input with speech. The mic's USB port can be used briefly for one-time signaling setup.

## Brand Commitments

Minimal, clean and NASA control inspired. The interface should show whether the audio input, control link and shortcut are ready.
